A little about us

A coffee shop first.
Something extra, too.

We’re a neighborhood coffee shop with shelves full of board games, cards, dice, and chess sets. Some people come in every week to play. Some haven't touched a board game since they were ten. Both are completely welcome.

Grab a maple latte, settle into one of our mismatched chairs, pull up to the community table, and see what happens. You might play something you already know. You might learn something new. You might just have coffee and get some work done.
