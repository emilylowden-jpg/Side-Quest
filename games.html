New to tabletop games?

You don't need to know the rules before you walk in.

Seriously. Some of our regulars play every week. Others haven't played anything since Monopoly when they were ten. There is room for both.

If you're curious, come by during Learn-a-Game Night on the first Wednesday of each month from 7:00 PM to 8:30 PM. Someone from our staff will teach a game from the shelf.
